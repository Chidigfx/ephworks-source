# Vouching rules

A vouch is a church saying "we know this person". It is a yes or no, nothing more: we never record faith, denomination or how well they know the person. Every rule below is enforced by the database, not just the screen.

## Why we vouch

A job can only go live once the person who posted it has been vouched for by a partner church. This is our main protection against fake employers.

## Who can vouch

- **Only a church admin**, and only for people who asked **their** church. A church admin at another church can't see or answer the request.
- **Nobody vouches for themselves**, church admins included.
- **Nobody else** can vouch: moderators, admins, volunteers and pastoral carers don't have the power.
- Church admins can only answer **yes or no**. They can't edit what the person wrote.
- Church admins are appointed by our admins after they apply ("Apply to help"). An admin usually checks with a short call first.

## How a vouch is asked for

1. A member who wants to post a job picks their church and sends a short request (name, optional note).
2. The church admin sees it under **Church vouches** and taps **Vouch for member** or **Decline**.
3. If the member changes their request, it goes back to the church as a new request.

## Undoing a wrong vouch

- The church admin opens **Church vouches** and taps **Withdraw vouch** on that person.
- Straight away, **all their live jobs leave the board**: nobody can see them, mark interest or get alerts about them. Nothing is deleted.
- If the church vouches again, the jobs come back until they expire (30 days after publishing).
- New posts from that person can't be published while they're not vouched.

## Removing a bad account

- An **admin suspends** the account on the **People** page. A suspended account can't post, vouch, report, apply or ask for anything, and their live jobs disappear from the board.
- Lifting the suspension restores everything.
- A moderator can also **remove a single job** at any time.
- To delete an account and its data completely, the person (or an admin on their behalf) sends a request through **Contact us**. It's done by hand in the database, and everything linked to the account is deleted with it.

## Who decides what

| Decision | Who |
|---|---|
| Vouch, decline or withdraw a vouch | That church's church admin |
| Publish or remove a job | Moderators and admins |
| Suspend or restore an account | Admins |
| Appoint church admins | Admins |
| Appoint or remove admins | The super admin only |

## Known gaps

- Only the church admin can withdraw a vouch. If they're unavailable, an admin's only option is to suspend the account.
- We don't keep a history of past vouches and withdrawals, only the current answer and who gave it.
